# Challenge brief

## Event

**Build an Agent Hackathon**  
Wednesday, October 7, 2026 · 2:00–5:30 PM PDT  
Corgi Cafe, 9 Claude Ln, San Francisco  
Remote participation is available through the event Zoom.

Organized by **Agent 37 (YC F26), InsForge (YC P26) and Monid**. Hosted at Corgi Cafe.

## Sponsors

- **Agent37** — required cloud/API platform for the agent.
- **OpenAI** — sponsor and model/API credits.
- **Supabase** — sponsor and backend/data credits.
- **InstaCloud / InsForge** — agent-native deployment and infrastructure.
- **Monid** — agent tool/API access layer.

Prize eligibility requires Agent37 Cloud APIs and at least one sponsor in the app. The event page says: “The more, the better.”

## Challenge language

The event asks:

> “What's the workflow you never want to do again?”

> “Build an agent that takes over repetitive, frustrating work you'd happily never touch again.”

> “The goal isn't just a cool demo.”

> “Build agents you can sell, and make something people or agents want.”

> “Replace your most annoying workflow.”

> “Replace yourself with an agent.”

> “Win your time back.”

The event gives these examples of the target:

- “the workflow you procrastinate every week”
- “a SaaS subscription you can replace”
- “the thing you'd hire someone just to never do again”

## Required use

- Use Agent37 Cloud APIs.
- Use at least one sponsor in the application.
- Additional sponsor integrations are encouraged.

## Submission

One teammate submits for the team. The submission includes:

- Team members.
- The workflow the agent replaces.
- A demo video link.
- Sponsor integrations.
- Live project or repository links.
- Up to five files, each up to 10 MB.

Judges must be able to open submitted links without requesting access. Remote submissions are judged asynchronously.
